The homepage service cards use 16:9 WebP images in `public/brand/home-service-cards/`.
The images illustrate the services described on the cards.

| Cards | Framer Marketplace source |
| --- | --- |
| Landing pages and redesigns | [Brog](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/brog/) |
| Custom software, reporting dashboards | [Alytics](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/alytics/) |
| CRM, unfinished projects | [FlowSuite](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/flowsuite/) |

The remaining cards use original mockups generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.
The common prompt called for wide artwork for a small studio service card, with
paper white, ice blue, graphite and cobalt, crisp edges and enough breathing room
for cropping. Each image used a different subject and Framer reference.
