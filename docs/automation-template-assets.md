# Automation page template images

The service-card visuals are local copies of internal dashboard and data assets from the live [Predict AI template](https://predict.framer.ai/) and [Torch SaaS template](https://torch-template.framer.website/), discovered through their [Framer Marketplace listings](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/predict/) and [Torch listing](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/torch/).

They are stored under `public/brand/services/automation/` and do not load from a remote CDN at runtime. The selected assets show mobile insights, dashboards, metric cards, trend lines, and reporting views instead of Marketplace listing screenshots.
